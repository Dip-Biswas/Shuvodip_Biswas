How to play

Flick. On your turn, drag back anywhere on the desk. The arrow shows where your pen will go. Release to shoot.

Knock out. A pen falls when its centre crosses the desk edge. If both pens fall on one flick, replay the round.

Use the keyboard. Focus the desk. Left and right adjust aim, up and down adjust power, and Space flicks.
